MY PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

From solving technical problems to understanding the bigger picture.

My journey in IT started with a simple goal: understand how technology works and use it to solve real problems. Along the way, I discovered that good IT support is not only about fixing computers or networks. It is also about understanding people, processes, and the business behind the technology.

My experience as an IT Support Specialist allowed me to work beyond day-to-day technical troubleshooting. I became involved in gathering business requirements, documenting system needs, and translating those requirements into clear documentation for technical and non-technical teams.

I worked with Business Requirements Documents, Functional Requirements Specifications, User Stories, BPMN, ArchiMate, and UML Use Case Diagrams to map existing processes and identify opportunities for improvement and automation.

I also performed CRUD analysis, prepared User Acceptance Testing scripts, guided end-users during testing, and coordinated with developers to verify software fixes before project rollout.

Alongside business and system analysis, I continued working hands-on with computers, hardware, software, peripherals, LAN connectivity, basic network configuration, and CCTV systems.

My experience as a Technical Specialist introduced me to technical compliance matrices, client discovery meetings, pre-bid conferences, solution design, proposal documentation, and translating client requirements into workflows and system diagrams.

My IT foundation was built during my internship at the University of Batangas IT Center , where I provided frontline IT support, performed hardware maintenance, configured LAN setups, created UI prototypes using Figma and Framer, and developed basic responsive web pages using HTML and CSS.
